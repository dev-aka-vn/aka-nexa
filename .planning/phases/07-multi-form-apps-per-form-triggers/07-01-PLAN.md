<objective>
The atomic stored-shape cutover: an app stops being "one embedded `form_schema` + a flat app-level
`triggers` array" and becomes a **container of forms** — `forms: [{ form_id, kind, schema,
form_version, triggers | null, config }]` — with the whole tree compiling, the whole vitest suite
green, and all 10 seeds loading. This is the phase's tracer: one path (seed JSON →
`TemplateSeed.load()` → fork → app document → publish gate → SPA save) through every layer the
form set touches.

Purpose: every later plan (section retirement, seed enrichment, the two-collection migration,
the builder manager, form-driven routing) builds on this shape; nothing else can land until it
exists.
Output: the `forms[]` contract + fixture, the migrated domain document/repository/service/gate,
rewritten seeds, and the SPA wizard/designer saving through the `forms` section.

<assumption_delta_decision>
Noun promoted: the **form** (identified by `form_id` + `kind`, carrying its own `form_version`
and its own trigger); the app is demoted to container (owner of the collection, roles, and
routing destination). Decision: **promote** — `forms[]` *replaces* `form_schema` + app-level
`triggers`; adding the plural alongside the singular is explicitly rejected because it would
leave `form_schema` as a second, competing source of truth for "the app's form". Accepted debt:
none. Invariant test (added in 07-08): every form in an app round-trips through the shared
`(app, action)` routing projection for every kind — it goes red the moment a future phase
reintroduces a single-form assumption.
</assumption_delta_decision>
</objective>
