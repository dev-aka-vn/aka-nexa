## Phase Goal

> Same derivation as plan 01: the ROADMAP goal is not in user-story format, so it is restated, not invented. **As a** platform engineer, **I want to** have every structured log line rebuilt from a closed field allowlist before it is serialised, **so that** no email, name, chat text, or submission body can reach production logs and the narrowed erasure promise is true.

</objective>

| log record → serialised bytes | The boundary that matters: after `JSON.stringify`, PII cannot be recalled. |
| error object → log record | A downstream HTTP error body may carry PII through an error `cause` chain. |
