<objective>
Deliver LNK-02: an owner-only edit link on the frozen `edit` action that can be reopened repeatedly, is consumed once on submit, expires after a configurable TTL (default 4 hours), and returns the original confirmation on a second submit (SC#4). Single-use is claimed by the atomic check-and-create of a new revision (partial unique index over `edit_jti`) committed with its outbox row, so a failed submit consumes nothing; a submitted edit is a new revision with a new send.

Purpose: SC#4 — the edit link is the user-facing half of the per-revision outbound model; a non-atomic consume or a duplicated revision here is a silent downstream double-write.
Output: the `edit` verifier branch, an atomic one-time token store with an outcome key, the edit controller path, and their proofs.
</objective>
