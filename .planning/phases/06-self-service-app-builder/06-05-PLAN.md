## Phase Goal

**As a** department admin, **I want to** build, secure, and publish a working app end-to-end, **so that** I need no engineering ticket and no platform admin's help.

<objective>
Deliver the publish gate: the APP-07 routing dry-run (executed through the real Phase-3 routing machinery, read-only, blocking any publish that drops an existing app's routing share by more than 5 points), the APP-08 description-collision check, the BLD-06 change summary (the publish confirmation payload), and the OBS-05 `routing_share{app_id}` metric exported through the single OTel metrics path.

Purpose: publishing is the moment a mistake becomes live. The gate is the last check before an app's triggers enter the routing distribution, and the metric is the standing signal that detects routing traffic theft after publish. Both consume the same per-intent outcome records, so the gate's prediction and the exported metric cannot disagree (Pitfall 9).

Output: `PublishGateService` with the `PublishGateResult` contract, the routing-outcome record store, the share computer, the OTel instrument, `POST /apps/:id/publish`, and the Zod publish contracts.
</objective>
