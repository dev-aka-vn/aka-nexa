## Phase Goal

> Same derivation as plan 01: the ROADMAP goal is not in user-story format, so it is restated, not invented. **As a** platform operator, **I want to** have `/health/live` and per-dependency `/health/ready` on every process, **so that** a Redis or Mongo blip fails readiness without restarting a healthy pod, and the failing dependency is named.

<objective>
Build the data-access floor and the health topology. Ship `MongoService` over the native `mongodb`
driver, per-dependency terminus indicators for Mongo and each Redis instance, and a `HealthController`
where liveness reads no dependency and readiness always checks Mongo, cache Redis, and queue Redis.
Prove the three-container topology with testcontainers: two Redis instances with different
`maxmemory-policy` values and different host:port.

Purpose: FND-05 is the observable floor later phases deploy against. D-10 makes readiness
per-dependency so an operator can tell *which* Redis is down; D-07 makes the container topology three
because `maxmemory-policy` is instance-wide and one container cannot express the split.

Output: a booted Mongo connection, a per-dependency readiness surface, and the integration harness
that proves the two-Redis split is real.
</objective>
