## Phase Goal

**As a** department admin, **I want to** build, secure, and publish a working app end-to-end, **so that** I need no engineering ticket and no platform admin's help.

<objective>
Deliver the builder's remaining surfaces per D-109: the read-only Dashboard (live data only, D-112), the Submissions page (viewer + detail + export link), the Connectors page (status badges, the D-99 request form, cancel/retry), the Users page (IDN-08 SSO mapping + ACL-05 transfer approval with the 7-day auto-cancel), the read-only Audit Log (AUD-01 browse), the Apps gallery (APP-09 tag filter), and the Settings stub (D-109 deferred).

</objective>
